For Buyers

  • Post Demands
  • Track Purchases
  • Demand Trends
P2P Marketplace Disclaimers

Direct Payments: FarmConnect does not collect or hold payments. Payments are made directly to the farmer via Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer.

Logistics: Transportation is coordinated directly between the farmer and buyer.
