SIH 2026 Problem Statement: SIH26033

About FarmConnect AI

FarmConnect AI is an AI-powered digital agriculture marketplace engineered to directly link smallholder farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) with commercial bulk buyers, food processing units, and wholesalers.

The Problem We Solve

Small farmers face low price realization due to multi-tiered middleman commission networks, lack of local demand visibility, and asymmetric market information. FarmConnect AI creates a transparent direct peer-to-peer ecosystem with explainable suitability scoring.

Our Core Principles
  • Zero Commission P2P: FarmConnect does not collect or hold transactions; payments are settled directly between farmer and buyer via Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer.
  • Transparent AI Matching: Clear, human-readable match factors (Crop, Quantity, Budget, Haversine Distance, and Harvest Dates).
  • 100% Real Database Integrity: All listings, demand insights, and transaction metrics are dynamically calculated from MySQL records.
